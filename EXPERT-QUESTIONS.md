# Expert Question Register

## EQ-0001 â€” Integration of structural and neurological foot findings

**Question:** On the documentary record, should the recurrent corns, callus, clawing, altered loading and focal foot pain be regarded as wholly independent structural pathology, or could Parkinsonian rigidity, dystonia, abnormal gait or altered loading have materially caused, aggravated or perpetuated them?

**If independent:** The structural foot disorder remains a separate explanatory stream and neurological treatment would not be expected to resolve its underlying cause.

**If neurologically caused/aggravated:** The foot pathology forms part of, or is materially downstream from, the movement disorder and should have been integrated into neurological assessment and management.

**Engages:** PROP-0001, PROP-0002, PROP-0011

## EQ-0002 â€” Significance of the 2017 procyclidine response

**Question:** What clinical significance should reasonably have been attached to the dramatic functional improvement on procyclidine and the documented deterioration in tension, feet and gait during withdrawal in 2017?

**If diagnostically significant:** The response supplied contemporaneous evidence that at least part of the disputed gait/foot presentation was neurologically modifiable and warranted reassessment of symptom attribution.

**If only symptomatic/non-specific:** The response would not materially distinguish a neurological generator from secondary effects on tone, loading, discomfort or movement.

**Engages:** PROP-0003, PROP-0004

## EQ-0003 â€” Duty to revisit the original symptom partition

**Question:** As medication-responsive foot symptoms, parkinsonian foot phenomena, OFF-related pain/walking difficulty and later clinical reinterpretations accumulated, was it clinically reasonable to continue treating the foot problem as substantially separate without an explicit reassessment of that partition?

**If yes:** The documentary sequence remains compatible with reasonable parallel management of co-existing structural and neurological problems.

**If no:** The record identifies a point at which the earlier separation required active reconsideration and integration.

**Engages:** PROP-0001, PROP-0004, PROP-0011

## EQ-0004 â€” Proportionality of levodopa deferral

**Question:** Given the patient's very young onset, documented dyskinesia/impulse-control risk, functional impairment and subsequent treatment history, was the strategy of deferring levodopa from 2017 until 2021 clinically proportionate throughout that period?

**If proportionate:** The delay represented a defensible risk-minimisation strategy in a highly treatment-sensitive young patient.

**If not proportionate:** Expert evidence should identify when the balance shifted such that the functional cost of continued deferral outweighed the anticipated long-term treatment risks.

**Engages:** PROP-0005, PROP-0006, PROP-0007, PROP-0010

## EQ-0005 â€” Earlier levodopa and probable functional effect

**Question:** If levodopa had been introduced earlier, is it more likely than not that it would have materially improved gait, rigidity, dystonia, foot-related disability or pain during the period of deferral?

**If yes:** The expert should identify which symptoms were likely responsive, the likely degree of benefit, and the earliest clinically reasonable treatment point.

**If no or uncertain:** The documentary record cannot support a counterfactual claim of meaningful earlier functional improvement from levodopa.

**Engages:** PROP-0006, PROP-0007, PROP-0010

## EQ-0006 â€” Meaning of later dyskinesia for the earlier rationale

**Question:** How should the later statement that Sinemet may fuel or reveal dyskinesia but does not cause it be reconciled with the earlier strategy of preserving levodopa for as long as possible because of dyskinesia risk?

**If consistent:** The later statement can be understood as a refinement of the same clinically reasonable concern about exposing an underlying susceptibility.

**If materially inconsistent:** The expert should explain whether the earlier deferral rationale overstated the protective value of delaying levodopa.

**Engages:** PROP-0005, PROP-0006, PROP-0008

## EQ-0007 â€” Significance of actual dopaminergic adverse effects

**Question:** To what extent do the documented chorea/dyskinesia and compulsive-behaviour concerns on dopaminergic treatment validate the earlier cautious treatment strategy, and to what extent do they merely establish that careful dosing and monitoring were required?

**If they validate prolonged deferral:** The later adverse effects support the original longitudinal strategy.

**If they support cautious treatment rather than prolonged avoidance:** The existence of risk would not itself justify continuing substantial undertreatment.

**Engages:** PROP-0006, PROP-0007, PROP-0010

## EQ-0010 â€” Significance of medication-related relief of foot symptoms

**Question:** What weight should be given to the repeated documentary association between Parkinson's medication state and relief or worsening of foot symptoms, including later reports that the feet improved once medication took effect?

**If mechanistically significant:** This supports a neurological or mixed neurological-biomechanical contribution.

**Engages:** PROP-0003, PROP-0004, PROP-0011

**If yes:** The expert should identify the likely onset and clinical consequences of undertreatment.
