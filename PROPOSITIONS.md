## PROP-0001 â€” The feet cannot be treated as a wholly separate evidential stream

The documentary record contains both genuine structural foot pathology and repeated neurological/treatment-state associations. ## PROP-0002 â€” Structural pathology does not exclude a neurological generator or contributor

Corns, callus, clawing, pronation and local pressure lesions establish structural pathology, but they do not by themselves determine the upstream cause of abnormal loading, recurrent pressure or pain. The record therefore supports a distinction between the existence of structural lesions and the mechanism producing or aggravating them.

## PROP-0003 â€” The 2017 treatment response was clinically informative

The dramatic improvement on procyclidine and deterioration during withdrawal were contemporaneously documented and involved gait, tension, feet and broader motor function. Whatever the ultimate mechanism, this pattern was evidence capable of informing subsequent diagnostic and management reasoning and was not merely retrospective recollection.

## PROP-0005 â€” Levodopa deferral was a deliberate longitudinal strategy

The record does not show an accidental omission of levodopa. It shows a conscious strategy, beginning in 2017, to postpone levodopa because of very young onset and concern about dyskinesia and behavioural complications.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0004, TENSION-0005

## PROP-0006 â€” The relevant expert issue is proportionality of deferral, not whether risk existed

The later occurrence of dyskinesia/chorea and behavioural concerns confirms that dopaminergic sensitivity was real. The unresolved clinical question is therefore not whether those risks existed, but whether the duration and intensity of treatment restraint remained proportionate to the patient's evolving disability, pain, OFF symptoms and functional burden.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0004, TENSION-0005, TENSION-0007

## PROP-0007 â€” Later treatment experience does not retrospectively prove that earlier levodopa should have been started

The record supports benefit from dopaminergic therapy and later concern about undertreatment, but it also documents adverse effects and a difficult therapeutic balance. The documentary case therefore supports an expert question about timing and dosing; it does not itself establish that immediate or substantially earlier levodopa was necessarily required.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0004, TENSION-0005, TENSION-0007

## PROP-0008 â€” The 2022 explanation creates a material tension with the earlier rationale for prolonged avoidance

The later statement that Sinemet may fuel or reveal dyskinesia but does not cause it is clinically relevant to the earlier strategy of preserving levodopa for as long as possible. Expert evidence is required to determine whether this represents a genuine change in scientific understanding, a refinement of the earlier rationale, or a material inconsistency in management reasoning.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0005

## PROP-0009 â€” Genetic suspicion was potentially management-relevant

The contemporaneous PARK2 suspicion was not merely classificatory: Healy linked it to a particular pattern of dopamine-replacement tolerance and dyskinesia risk. The decision to defer genetic confirmation therefore raises an expert question about whether genotype could reasonably have informed prognosis, counselling or treatment strategy.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0006

## PROP-0010 â€” The documentary record supports a real risk of undertreatment

By 2021 the patient reported feeling undertreated, and by 2023 the treating team considered insufficient levodopa a plausible explanation for significant walking difficulty and OFF-related symptoms. These entries establish undertreatment as a live clinical issue requiring expert assessment, even though they do not by themselves establish negligent treatment.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0007

## PROP-0011 â€” Pain requires a mechanism-based analysis rather than a binary Parkinsonian/non-Parkinsonian label

The record supports several potentially overlapping mechanisms: focal structural lesions, abnormal loading, dystonia, altered gait, OFF-state worsening and broader pain modulation by dopaminergic treatment. A binary classification of the pain as either Parkinson's disease or entirely separate risks obscuring the mixed mechanisms documented over time.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0001, TENSION-0002, TENSION-0008

## PROP-0012 â€” The strongest documentary case turns on unanswered expert questions

The documents establish chronology, treatment decisions, treatment responses, structural pathology and later reinterpretations. They do not themselves resolve causation, standard of care, counterfactual outcome or whether earlier intervention would probably have altered the course. Those conclusions depend on expert neurological and, where necessary, biomechanical/podiatric opinion.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0001 through TENSION-0008
