# Clinical Proposition Register

## PROP-0001 — The feet cannot be treated as a wholly separate evidential stream

The documentary record contains both genuine structural foot pathology and repeated neurological/treatment-state associations. Any clinical account that treats the feet as entirely independent of the movement disorder must explain the documented medication-linked recurrence, parkinsonian foot throw, OFF-related pain and shuffling, and later acknowledgement that Parkinson's disease could account for much of the presentation.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0001, TENSION-0002, TENSION-0008

## PROP-0002 — Structural pathology does not identify its own upstream cause

Corns, callus, clawing, pronation and local pressure lesions establish structural pathology, but they do not determine why abnormal loading or recurrent pressure arose. In this record, structural lesions may have been independent, neurologically aggravated, or partly downstream from dystonia, rigidity or altered gait.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0001, TENSION-0008

## PROP-0003 — The 2017–2018 treatment-response sequence was clinically informative

The improvement on procyclidine and deterioration during withdrawal were contemporaneously documented, and the 2018 neurologist recorded that the first rapidly changing feature during withdrawal was the return of the outside-left-foot phenomenon. Whatever the mechanism, this was evidence capable of informing subsequent symptom attribution and management.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0003

## PROP-0004 — The adequacy of the original symptom partition required continuing reassessment

As medication-responsive foot symptoms, explicitly parkinsonian lower-limb phenomena, OFF-related pain/shuffling and later clinical reinterpretations accumulated, any earlier division between Parkinsonian and separate foot symptoms became progressively more dependent on explicit reassessment rather than passive continuation.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0002, TENSION-0003, TENSION-0008

## PROP-0005 — Levodopa deferral was a deliberate longitudinal strategy

The record does not show an accidental omission of levodopa. It shows a conscious strategy, beginning in 2017, to postpone levodopa because of very young onset and concern about dyskinesia and behavioural complications.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0004, TENSION-0005

## PROP-0006 — The critical expert issue is proportionality of deferral over time

The existence of genuine dyskinesia and behavioural risk is not seriously in dispute. The unresolved question is whether the duration and intensity of treatment restraint remained proportionate to the patient's evolving disability, pain, OFF symptoms and functional burden at each stage.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0004, TENSION-0005, TENSION-0007, TENSION-0010

## PROP-0007 — Later adverse effects do not retrospectively prove that earlier restraint was proportionate

Subsequent chorea, dyskinesia and behavioural concerns validate treatment sensitivity, but they do not automatically establish that every earlier period of levodopa avoidance was clinically justified. The reasonableness of earlier decisions must be assessed primarily against what was known contemporaneously.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0007, TENSION-0010

## PROP-0008 — The record itself recognised substantial potential benefit from stronger dopaminergic treatment

The treating neurologist recorded both that clinicians might later regret not starting levodopa earlier and, in 2018, that the patient could be "very much better" if given levodopa or another dopamine agonist. These entries do not prove that earlier levodopa was required, but they establish that foregone symptomatic benefit was expressly recognised during the period of deferral.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0004, TENSION-0009

## PROP-0009 — Disease-progression neutrality does not answer symptomatic harm

The 2017 assertion that delaying symptomatic treatment would not worsen neuropathological progression addresses neurodegeneration. It does not answer whether prolonged deferral caused avoidable symptoms, loss of function, pain or reduced quality of life during the years before levodopa was introduced.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0009

## PROP-0010 — The 2022 delayed-start explanation creates a material expert issue

The later statement that Sinemet may fuel or reveal dyskinesia but does not cause it is clinically relevant to the earlier strategy of preserving levodopa for as long as possible. Expert evidence is required to determine whether this was consistent with the original rationale, a refinement of it, or evidence that the protective value of prolonged deferral had been overstated.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0005

## PROP-0011 — Genetic suspicion was potentially management-relevant

The contemporaneous PARK2 suspicion was not merely classificatory. Healy linked it to low-dose dopamine-replacement management and dyskinesia risk, while the treating neurologist separately acknowledged possible relevance to prognostication and levodopa sensitivities and accepted there was reasonable logic to testing immediately.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0006

## PROP-0012 — The documentary record supports a real episode or risk of undertreatment

By 2021 the patient reported feeling undertreated. By 2023 the consultant suspected lack of levodopa was contributing materially to evening walking difficulty, and the Parkinson's nurse expressly assessed the patient as undertreated with OFF-related leg pain and shuffling. These entries make undertreatment a documentary issue requiring expert assessment.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0007

## PROP-0013 — Pain requires a mechanism-based analysis rather than a binary label

The record supports potentially overlapping mechanisms: focal structural lesions, abnormal loading, dystonia, altered gait, OFF-state worsening, central or non-motor pain modulation and secondary musculoskeletal effects. Dopaminergic relief is relevant but not specific, as the 2025 neurologist expressly cautioned.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0001, TENSION-0008

## PROP-0014 — The strongest documentary case turns on the timing of reassessment

The key longitudinal question is not merely whether the original 2017 judgments were defensible when made. It is whether, and when, later evidence required a change in symptom attribution, investigation or treatment strategy.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0002, TENSION-0004, TENSION-0007, TENSION-0009

## PROP-0015 — The documentary record does not itself resolve standard of care or counterfactual outcome

The documents establish chronology, treatment decisions, treatment responses, structural pathology, later reinterpretations and recognised treatment trade-offs. They do not themselves establish negligence, the standard of care, the precise causal mechanism of pain, or the outcome that earlier levodopa or genetic testing would probably have produced.

**Derived from:** TENSION-0001 through TENSION-0010
