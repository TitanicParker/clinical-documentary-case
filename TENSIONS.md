## TENSION-0003 â€” Treatment response as symptom control vs treatment response as diagnostic information

**Documentary tension:** Procyclidine produced profound global functional improvement in 2017, and withdrawal was followed by worsening tension, restless feet and deterioration of gait. These observations were contemporaneous. The record establishes treatment responsiveness but does not itself determine the mechanism or prove that levodopa would have produced the same effect.

**Anchored facts:** FACT-0011, FACT-0013, FACT-0014, FACT-0015

**Expert issue:** What diagnostic weight, if any, should have been given to the pattern of improvement and withdrawal deterioration, particularly in relation to the disputed foot and gait phenomena?

## TENSION-0004 â€” Levodopa deferral for long-term risk vs contemporaneous functional benefit foregone

**Documentary tension:** From 2017 onward the record shows a deliberate strategy to defer levodopa because of very young onset and concern about dyskinesia and impulse-control complications. The same record also acknowledged that levodopa might produce substantial benefit and that clinicians might later regret not starting it earlier. Levodopa was not introduced until 2021, after the patient reported feeling undertreated.

**Anchored facts:** FACT-0009, FACT-0010, FACT-0012, FACT-0018, FACT-0019

**Expert issue:** Was the duration and degree of levodopa deferral clinically proportionate to the documented risks, symptoms and functional burden at each stage?

## TENSION-0005 â€” Dyskinesia risk rationale vs later delayed-start explanation

**Documentary tension:** The clinical strategy repeatedly relied on concern that dopaminergic treatment in a very young patient would precipitate or accelerate troublesome dyskinesia. Actual chorea/dyskinesia later occurred with dopamine agonist and levodopa exposure, supporting genuine sensitivity. In 2022, however, the neurologist recorded that delayed-start evidence indicated Sinemet may fuel or reveal dyskinesia but does not cause it, attributing the underlying cause to impaired cerebral dopamine handling.

**Anchored facts:** FACT-0012, FACT-0016, FACT-0017, FACT-0018, FACT-0021, FACT-0022, FACT-0023

**Expert issue:** How should the early deferral rationale be evaluated in light of later evidence and the distinction between causing dyskinesia and revealing/fuelling an underlying susceptibility?

## TENSION-0006 â€” Genetic suspicion and treatment relevance vs deferred genetic testing

**Documentary tension:** In 2017 a specialist considered PARK2 sufficiently likely to recommend targeted testing, and specifically linked PARK2 with prolonged management on relatively low doses of dopamine-replacement therapy. The clinical record nevertheless contemplated delaying genetic testing, partly because it was thought not to change current management.

**Anchored facts:** FACT-0010, FACT-0011, FACT-0012

**Expert issue:** Given the contemporaneous suspicion of PARK2 and the stated possible relationship between genotype, prognosis and dopamine sensitivity, could genetic confirmation reasonably have altered management, counselling or treatment strategy?

## TENSION-0007 â€” Demonstrated dopaminergic sensitivity vs later evidence of undertreatment

**Documentary tension:** The record documents genuine difficulty balancing benefit against adverse dopaminergic effects, including compulsive-behaviour concerns and chorea/dyskinesia. It also later records that the patient felt undertreated, that levodopa was started only in 2021, and that by 2023 the treating team considered lack of levodopa a plausible explanation for substantial evening walking difficulty.

**Anchored facts:** FACT-0016, FACT-0017, FACT-0019, FACT-0021, FACT-0022, FACT-0024

**Expert issue:** Did the treatment strategy maintain an appropriate balance between avoiding adverse effects and preventing clinically significant OFF symptoms, pain and loss of function?
